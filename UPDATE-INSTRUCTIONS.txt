APPLY TO YOUR EXISTING REPOSITORY (following V5.8.6):

2. Extract this ZIP.
3. Copy index.html, _next/ and pp-byzantine/ to the repository ROOT.

CHANGES
• Clicking Portal in the menu returns to the portal, resetting the stored
  crystal selection. Entering from the portal always opens crystal #1.
• Removes Contact from both navigation menus, keyboard end navigation, and
  route entry, and stops scroll at the end of the About area so the retired
  contact experience cannot be reached.
• Removes public Lite Version links, options and fallback redirects.
  Unsupported WebGPU browsers show a clear requirements notice instead.
• Replaces four PNG backgrounds with LOSSLESS WebP equivalents: visible RGBA
  pixels are identical. All image plane positions and shaders are unchanged.
• Preserves V5.8.6 portal traversal animation and back-scroll lock, and all
  four crystals, terrain shaders, landmark deformation and timing.

claim that the website is fully optimized across every device or GPU.

untouched to preserve the approved visual effects. Contact assets remaining
in the existing repository can be reviewed later before deletion.
